AI-Powered KPI Analysis Dashboard

Project Overview

This project analyzes marketing campaign performance using Python and KPI-driven business analytics. The objective is to evaluate campaign effectiveness, identify high-performing marketing channels, and generate actionable business insights for data-driven decision-making.

Dataset Information

The dataset contains 55,555 marketing campaign records with information such as:

- Campaign Type
- Target Audience
- Channel Used
- Impressions
- Clicks
- Leads
- Conversions
- Revenue
- Acquisition Cost
- ROI
- Engagement Score
- Customer Segment
- Date

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Revenue
- Total Campaigns
- Conversion Rate
- Click Through Rate (CTR)
- Average ROI

Visualizations

- Revenue by Campaign Type – Analyzes revenue generated across different marketing campaign types.
- Average ROI by Campaign Type – Compares campaign efficiency and return on investment.
- Conversion Rate by Campaign Type – Evaluates how effectively campaigns convert clicks into conversions.
- Monthly Revenue Trend – Tracks revenue performance over time to identify trends.
- Revenue by Customer Segment – Identifies the most valuable customer groups based on revenue contribution.

AI-Powered Features

Campaign Health Score

A custom performance score calculated using:

- Revenue
- ROI
- Engagement Score

This helps identify the strongest overall campaign type.

AI Recommendation Engine

Automatically recommends the best-performing campaign strategy based on KPI performance.

Key Business Insights

1. Social Media campaigns achieved the highest ROI, making them the most cost-effective marketing channel.
2. Email campaigns achieved the highest Conversion Rate, demonstrating strong customer conversion efficiency.
3. Influencer campaigns generated the highest total revenue among all campaign types.
4. Social Media campaigns achieved the highest Campaign Health Score.
5. Working Women generated the highest revenue among customer segments.
6. Revenue distribution remained balanced across campaign types, indicating a diversified marketing strategy.

Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

Project Outcome

Developed an AI-powered KPI analysis solution capable of evaluating marketing campaign performance, generating business insights, and supporting strategic decision-making through data analytics.
