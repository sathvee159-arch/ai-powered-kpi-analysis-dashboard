AI-Powered KPI Analysis and Campaign Performance Dashboard
Project Overview
This project analyzes marketing campaign performance using Python and KPI-based business analytics. The objective is to evaluate campaign effectiveness, identify high-performing channels, and generate actionable business insights.
Dataset
The dataset contains 55,555 marketing campaign records with information such as:
Campaign Type
Target Audience
Impressions
Clicks
Conversions
Revenue
Acquisition Cost
ROI
Engagement Score
Customer Segment
Date
KPIs Analyzed
Total Revenue
Total Campaigns
Click Through Rate (CTR)
Conversion Rate
Average ROI
Visualizations
Revenue by Campaign Type
Average ROI by Campaign Type
Conversion Rate by Campaign Type
Monthly Revenue Trend
Revenue by Customer Segment
AI Features
Campaign Health Score
A custom performance score calculated using:
Revenue
ROI
Engagement Score
AI Recommendation Engine
Automatically identifies the best-performing campaign type and provides business recommendations.
Key Business Insights
Social Media campaigns achieved the highest ROI.
Email campaigns achieved the highest Conversion Rate.
Influencer campaigns generated the highest Revenue.
Social Media campaigns achieved the highest Health Score.
Working Women generated the highest customer-segment revenue.
Revenue distribution remained balanced across campaign types.

Python

Project Outcome
Developed a KPI-driven marketing analytics solution capable of evaluating campaign performance, generating insights, and supporting data-driven decision making.
